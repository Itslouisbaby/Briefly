## IndieHackers Post

**Title:** Launched Briefly — AI daily briefings for busy professionals

**Body:**
Hey IH!

Just shipped Briefly (https://briefly.ai) — a daily intelligence briefing service that delivers personalized news briefings every morning.

**The Problem:**
I was tired of:
- Twitter algorithms deciding what I see
- News sites filled with clickbait
- Spending 30+ minutes just to stay informed
- Missing important stories in the noise

**The Solution:**
Briefly uses AI to curate a 5-minute daily briefing on the topics you care about (tech, markets, AI, science, world news). Choose text or audio, email or Slack. Get informed without the overwhelm.

**Pricing:**
- Basic: $9/mo (1 topic, text)
- Pro: $29/mo (3 topics, audio + text, Slack)
- Team: $99/mo (5 seats, API access)

**Tech Stack:**
- Node.js + Express
- SQLite (upgrading to Postgres)
- Stripe for billing
- ElevenLabs for voice
- Resend for email

**Current Status:**
- MVP live
- Looking for 10 beta users
- First 10 get 50% off forever

**Ask:**
Would love feedback on the landing page and pricing. Also looking for beta testers — reply if interested!
