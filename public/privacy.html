Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 4, 2026

Overview

At Briefly, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our daily intelligence briefing service.

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Email address, name, and billing information
  • Preferences: Topics you want to follow, delivery time, format preferences
  • Usage Data: How you interact with our briefings (opens, clicks, feedback)
  • Device Information: Browser type, IP address, device type

How We Use Your Information

  • Deliver personalized daily briefings to your inbox
  • Improve our AI curation based on your feedback
  • Process payments and manage your subscription
  • Send important service updates and notifications
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve our service

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
  • We use Stripe for payment processing - we never store your credit card details
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Limited employee access to user data on a need-to-know basis

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate Briefly:

  • Stripe - Payment processing
  • Resend - Email delivery
  • ElevenLabs - Audio generation (Pro/Team plans)
  • Slack - Optional delivery channel

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data
  • Opt out of non-essential communications

To exercise these rights, email us at privacy@briefly.io

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account:

  • Personal information is deleted within 30 days
  • Anonymized usage data may be retained for analytics
  • Legal obligations may require longer retention for some data

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@briefly.io
  • Address: Briefly Inc., San Francisco, CA
